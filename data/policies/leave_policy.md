# Leave Policy

Effective 1 April 2026.

## Annual Leave

- Full-time employees accrue **1.75 days per month**, totalling 21 days per year.
- A maximum of **10 unused days** may be carried into the next financial year.
  Anything above 10 lapses on 31 March.
- Leave must be applied for at least 3 working days in advance, except in
  emergencies.

## Sick Leave

- **12 days per financial year**, non-carryover.
- A medical certificate is required for absences of **3 consecutive days or more**.
- Sick leave cannot be encashed.

## Parental Leave

- Primary caregivers receive **26 weeks** of paid leave.
- Secondary caregivers receive **6 weeks** of paid leave, to be taken within
  12 months of the birth or adoption.
- Employees must have completed **6 months** of service to be eligible.

## Leave Encashment

- Only annual leave is encashable, and only on exit.
- Encashment is calculated as **(basic salary / 30) × number of unused days**,
  capped at **30 days** total.

## Unpaid Leave

Unpaid leave beyond 15 consecutive days requires department head approval and
pauses annual leave accrual for the duration.
