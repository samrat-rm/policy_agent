# Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy

Effective 1 April 2026. Applies to all full-time employees.

## Home Office Equipment

Employees working remotely may claim home office equipment.

- Monitors, keyboards, mice, and docking stations are reimbursed at **80% of
  the purchase price**, capped at **₹25,000 per item**.
- Office chairs are reimbursed at **60% of the purchase price**, capped at
  **₹15,000**.
- Desks are reimbursed at **60% of the purchase price**, capped at **₹20,000**.
- The total home office allowance is **₹60,000 per employee every two years**.

Claims must be submitted within 45 days of purchase with an original invoice.

## Travel

- Domestic economy airfare is reimbursed in full.
- Hotel stays are capped at **₹6,000 per night** in metro cities and
  **₹4,000 per night** elsewhere.
- Local travel by cab is reimbursed at actuals up to **₹1,500 per day**.
- Per-diem meal allowance is **₹1,200 per day** domestic and **$50 per day**
  international. Receipts are not required for per-diem.

## Learning and Development

- Employees may claim **₹40,000 per financial year** for courses,
  certifications, books, and conference tickets.
- Certification exam fees are reimbursed at **100%** on first attempt only.
  Retake fees are not reimbursed.
- Degree programmes require prior written approval from the department head
  and are reimbursed at **50%**, capped at **₹1,50,000 per year**.

## Internet and Phone

- Remote employees receive a flat **₹2,000 per month** internet allowance.
  No receipts required.
- Phone bills are reimbursed at actuals up to **₹1,000 per month** with a bill.

## Non-Reimbursable

The following are never reimbursed: personal entertainment, alcohol,
traffic fines, gym memberships, and travel upgrades to business class
without prior VP approval.
